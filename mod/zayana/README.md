# زيانه · Zayana

A cheerful Omani cartoon companion for Claude Code. Zayana bounces in a band above
the prompt, waves and blinks, and her speech bubble reacts to what Claude is doing:
reading files, running commands, writing code, and finishing a turn.

- `/zayana` brings her back, `/zayana hide` hides her, `/zayana pane` opens a large pane.
- On the desktop, web and mobile apps she is an animated SVG; in a terminal she is a line of text.
